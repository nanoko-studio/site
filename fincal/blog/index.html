Guides to loan and savings interest, with worked numbers and a calculator to try your own.

  • Fixed deposit interest: take it monthly or at maturity? Put 50,000 in a 12-month fixed deposit: what you get at maturity, what monthly interest adds up to if you save it again, and the monthly rate you need to break even. With chart, tables and calculator.
  • Paying off a loan early: shorter term or lower payment, and is the fee worth it? How much interest does a 20,000 overpayment on a 200,000 mortgage save, is a 2% early repayment charge worth paying, and should you shorten the term or lower the payment? With a chart, tables and a calculator.
  • When the intro rate ends: how much more will you pay each month? A 300,000 mortgage over 25 years at 4.5% fixed for two years, then a variable rate: how much the payment jumps in month 25, which deal is really cheaper, and how to prepare. With chart, tables and a calculator.
  • Flat rate vs reducing balance: which loan really costs more? A 12% flat rate costs almost twice as much as 12% on a reducing balance. See how each is worked out, a 10,000 loan example with charts, and a table that converts flat rates to the effective rate.
